Step by step ಹಂತ ಹಂತವಾಗಿ
  1. Article received. A gold or silver article comes in from a Hundi counting or as a Seva offering, and is weighed. Inventory Officer
  2. Open "Register Article" on a phone, tablet or computer and fill in six sections: receipt (date and time, Hundi or Seva, Hundi batch or Seva receipt number), donor (optional for Hundi), the article (gold or silver, category, type, description, number of pieces), weight and purity (gross and net weight to the milligram, purity, who weighed it, and the weight of each piece if there are several), photographs (camera or upload), and remarks. Inventory Officer
  3. Save. The application checks every required field. It then creates the Article ID automatically, records the entry as Pending verification, and writes an audit entry. Application
  4. Verify. A Verification Officer who did not enter the record opens it from the queue, checks the physical article, and ticks all six checks: description, photograph, quantity, weight on the scale, source of receipt, and donor details. Purity can be confirmed here. Verification Officer
  5. Approve or return. If everything matches, the officer approves it. The record is locked and counts in the gold and silver totals, the dashboard and all 12 reports. If something is wrong, the officer returns it with written remarks. Verification Officer
  6. Correct and resubmit. A returned entry appears on the Inventory Officer's dashboard with the remarks. After correction it goes back to Pending verification, and step 4 repeats. Inventory Officer
  7. Later corrections. An approved record can only be amended or cancelled by the Secretary (Super Admin), with a written reason. The old and new values are both kept. Nothing is ever deleted. Secretary

Safeguards ಸುರಕ್ಷತಾ ನಿಯಮಗಳು

  • The person who enters or edits a record can never verify it.
  • Approval needs all six checks ticked.
  • Approved records are locked. Only the Secretary can amend them, with a reason.
  • Records are cancelled with a reason, never deleted.
  • The audit trail cannot be edited or deleted, even by the Secretary.
  • Each officer has their own login. Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 wrong passwords.

Status of an entry ಸ್ಥಿತಿ

Pending verification Returned for correction Approved Cancelled

Reports can show approved entries only, or every entry including pending ones.
